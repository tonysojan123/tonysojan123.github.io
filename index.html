AI Wearable Audio Recorder & Note-taking Assistant

I designed and built a fully functional AI Wearable Audio Recorder & Note-taking Assistant. I set out to build a “second brain” — a device that could seamlessly capture daily conversations and meetings, then automatically generate organized transcripts and summaries. The goal was a frictionless user experience where capturing a thought requires zero screen time.

The solution is a pendant-style BLE wearable powered by a Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Sense. The device captures audio and streams it via Bluetooth Low Energy to the Omi iOS app, which performs transcription and generates summaries. This project was a fantastic challenge in end-to-end product prototyping, combining mechanical design, electronics fabrication, embedded firmware bring-up, and AI software integration.

Workflow

Power ON → BLE pair → capture conversation → transcript + summary generated in-app.

The Build Breakdown

  • ⚙️ Hardware: At the heart is the tiny but powerful Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Sense. I soldered and integrated a Li-Po battery and a slide switch into a custom, ultra-compact footprint, balancing physical size constraints with the power demands of continuous audio streaming.
  • 🔧 Mechanical: I designed a snap-fit enclosure in CAD and 3D printed it using a Prusa i3 MK3S+. Achieving the tight tolerances needed to securely house the electronics without using fasteners was a great iterative design process.
  • 🧠 Software & AI: The device uses BLE running Omi DevKit firmware to stream audio directly to the Omi AI iOS app, which handles real-time transcription and summarization.

What made this build so interesting was bringing together multiple disciplines — electronics assembly, additive manufacturing, embedded systems, and AI software integration — into a single palm-sized wearable.

Key tradeoffs

Small form factor and wearability vs battery life, BLE reliability, and enclosure tolerances.

Outcome

Successfully paired, captured a real conversation, and produced transcripts and summaries end-to-end.

Clarify AI: Your Guide to Better Buying

Clarify is an AI-powered browser extension designed to solve one of the most pervasive problems in modern online shopping: decision overload. As the digital commerce ecosystem grows more fragmented and saturated with unreliable reviews, sponsored content, and inconsistent product information, consumers increasingly struggle to make confident purchase decisions.

Clarify provides a simple but powerful solution: an AI-powered browser extension that consolidates product research into a single, accessible view. Instead of requiring users to jump between multiple tabs and platforms, Clarify analyzes information behind the scenes and then presents a clean summary directly on the product page.

Eco-Friendly Bicycle Helmet

Developed a sustainable composite cork material for helmet liners, specifically engineering a solution to replace traditional non-biodegradable foams. This project involved a rigorous R&D phase, including the selection of flexible binders and the design of custom test equipment to quantify energy absorption accurately.

The process required optimizing the manufacturing parameters—specifically binder concentration, molding temperature, and pressure—to achieve the ideal material properties. Through extensive compression and impact testing, I discovered that agglomerated cork combined with a polyurethane binder excels in crashworthiness, dimensional recovery, and mechanical performance, offering a highly effective and biodegradable alternative for eco-conscious protective gear.

AR/VR House Visualization Project

To address the gap between 2D blueprints and physical reality in architecture, I developed an immersive visualization tool using AR and VR technologies. This project enables homeowners and builders to experience a high-fidelity, interactive 3D model of a house before construction begins, significantly reducing costly design iterations and spatial conflicts.

The development followed two distinct tracks:
1. VR Implementation (Unity & Meta Quest 3): Created a fully immersive, interactive walkthrough allowing users to "be inside" the house to assess scale and spatial relationships.
2. Web-AR Implementation (Hoverlay): Enabled a full-scale 1:1 projection of the house model onto real-world construction sites via mobile phones, facilitating on-site visualization without dedicated headsets.
